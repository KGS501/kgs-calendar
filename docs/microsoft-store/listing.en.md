# Store listing: English

**Name:** KGS Calendar

**Short description**

A calendar and task app for Nextcloud, CalDAV and subscriptions, or simply on your PC.

**Description**

KGS Calendar brings your events and tasks together in one place. Connect your Nextcloud or another CalDAV server, subscribe to calendars, or keep a calendar on your PC without any account.

It is made for the desktop: a month overview next to your calendar, day, week, month, year and agenda views, tasks beside the calendar, right-click menus, and keyboard shortcuts you can change.

KGS Calendar has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. There is no KGS Calendar account, and your calendars only go to the servers you add.

**Features**

- Events and tasks in one app
- Day, multi-day, month, year and agenda views
- Sync with Nextcloud and CalDAV servers
- Read-only ICS and CalDAV subscriptions
- A local calendar without a server
- Reminders that stay in the notification centre
- Keeps running in the notification area, and can open when Windows starts
- Recurring events and tasks
- Import of ICS files
- Search across events and tasks
- Calendar colours, order and visibility
- Optional location search and map previews with OpenStreetMap
- Light and dark themes, and Windows' accent colour
- English and German
